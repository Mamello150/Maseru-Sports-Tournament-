About the Tournament

The Lesotho Sports Tournament is a community event of athlectic excellence, unity and Basotho pride. Every year teams compete across football, athletics, netball and volleyball. Our goal is to discover new talent, promote healthy living and strenghthen community bonds through sport. This even will take place at Setsoto Stadium in Maseru on 10 December 2026. Visitors will enjoy football matches, basketball games, athletics and entertainment activities throughout the day.
